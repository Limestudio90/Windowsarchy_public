# casse (speakers) - made for small desk speakers.
# Cuts the rumble they cannot reproduce (and that wastes excursion),
# removes the boom the desk surface adds around 180 Hz,
# and recovers some intelligibility and air.
Preamp: -4.0 dB
Filter: ON HPQ Fc 45 Hz Q 0.70
Filter: ON PK  Fc 180 Hz Gain -3.0 dB Q 1.20
Filter: ON PK  Fc 2500 Hz Gain 1.5 dB Q 1.00
Filter: ON HSC Fc 9000 Hz Gain 2.0 dB Q 0.70
